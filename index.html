Complete UAE Family Visa & Overstay Fine Rules (2026 Guidelines)

Comprehensive operational guidelines for UAE expatriates under GDRFA Dubai and ICP regulations. Calculate eligibility, understand legal salary thresholds, Ejari tenancy norms, and overstay fine structures.

1. Salary Limits & Eligibility Criteria

  • Family Sponsorship: Minimum gross salary of AED 4,000 OR AED 3,000 plus employer-provided housing.
  • Parents Sponsorship: Minimum monthly salary of AED 10,000 required. Both parents must be sponsored jointly under standard humanitarian provisions.
  • Female Resident Sponsorship: Women in qualifying professional roles can sponsor dependents upon meeting salary thresholds (AED 4,000–10,000 depending on Emirate).
  • Sponsor Labor Contract: Salary verification is strictly evaluated based on official MOHRE Labour Contract or Free Zone Salary Certificate.

2. Housing & Tenancy (Ejari Standards)

  • Ejari Contract Mandatory: Attested tenancy contracts (Ejari in Dubai / Tawtheeq in Abu Dhabi) under sponsor's or company's name are strictly required.
  • Parents Housing Standard: Sponsoring parents mandates a minimum 2-bedroom rental apartment (2-BHK).
  • Shared Accommodation Rules: Shared or sub-leased accommodation without municipal authorization leads to application rejection.

3. Overstay Fines & Grace Periods

  • Unified Daily Penalty: Standard overstay fine in the UAE is AED 50 per day for residence and visit visa overstays.
  • Grace Period After Cancellation: Expired or cancelled residence visa holders receive a 30 to 180-day grace period to exit or status-change without fines.
  • Outpass & Exit Clearance: Overstay fines must be cleared online or via immigration service centers prior to departure.

4. Medical Fitness & Health Insurance

  • Medical Fitness Test: Mandatory screening for HIV, Hepatitis B/C, Tuberculosis, and Syphilis for all dependents aged 18 and above.
  • Health Insurance Mandatory: Valid basic health insurance coverage is required for Emirates ID issuance across all Emirates.
  • Deposit Requirement: Deposit fees may apply when sponsoring parents or specific categories as security assurance.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. What is the minimum salary required to sponsor a family visa in Dubai in 2026?

The minimum monthly salary to sponsor a wife and children in the UAE is AED 4,000, or AED 3,000 if your employer provides free official housing with a registered Ejari.

2. What is the salary requirement to sponsor parents in the UAE?

To sponsor both parents, the resident must earn a minimum monthly salary of AED 10,000 and maintain a minimum 2-bedroom rental apartment under a valid Ejari tenancy contract.

3. How much is the daily fine for overstaying a residency or visit visa in the UAE?

Under the standardized ICP rules, the overstay fine is AED 50 per day for both visit visa holders and expired residence visa holders after the grace period.

4. What is the grace period after UAE residence visa cancellation?

Depending on your visa category and skill tier, the grace period ranges from 30 days to 180 days to either exit the country or modify your visa status without incurring overstay fines.

5. Is an Ejari contract mandatory for family visa renewal or new application?

Yes, an officially registered Ejari tenancy contract in Dubai (or Tawtheeq in Abu Dhabi) under the sponsor's or company's name is mandatory for GDRFA and ICP approvals.

6. Can a female employee sponsor her husband and children in UAE?

Yes, female residents employed in qualified professions can sponsor their family if they meet the minimum monthly salary threshold of AED 4,000 to AED 10,000 (depending on Emirate guidelines).

7. Is health insurance mandatory for dependents in UAE?

Yes, basic health insurance coverage is compulsory for all sponsored family members before the Emirates ID and residency visa stamp can be issued.

8. What medical tests are required for family visa applicants?

Dependents aged 18 and older must undergo medical fitness testing for communicable diseases, including HIV, Hepatitis B/C, Tuberculosis, and Syphilis at authorized health centers.

9. How to check UAE overstay visa fines online?

You can check overstay fines online using your Passport number or File number on the official ICP smart services portal (icp.gov.ae) or GDRFA Dubai portal.

10. Can I sponsor my stepchildren under UAE residence visa?

Yes, stepchildren can be sponsored subject to GDRFA approval, submission of a No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the biological parent, and a security deposit.
